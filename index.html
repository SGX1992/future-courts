Why GILTC

Why tennis?

Traditional networking creates dozens of short conversations. Tennis creates better ones — two people, thirty minutes, one thing to focus on. Competition breaks the ice, and the conversation follows naturally. The court is simply where the relationship begins.

Thirty minutes together

Every match puts two people side by side for half an hour — no name badges, no elevator pitch.

Competition breaks the ice

The game does the difficult part. By the first changeover, the conversation is already underway.

The court is the beginning

The match ends; the relationship does not. That is the entire point of the day.

Tournament format

A format where everyone plays.

Thirty-two players across eight courts, in a Swiss-style tournament of seven rounds. Winners meet winners; players with similar results meet each other. There is no early elimination — everyone plays throughout the day, right up to the championship final.

32 · 8

Thirty-two players, eight courts. A curated field, kept deliberately small.

Swiss · 7 rounds

Seven rounds, matched by result. You are always playing someone at your level.

No early exit

Every participant plays all day. One loss never ends your tournament.
