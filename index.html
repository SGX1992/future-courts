Why Future Courts

Why the court?

Traditional networking creates dozens of short conversations. Tennis, padel and pickleball create better ones — two people, thirty minutes, one thing to focus on. Competition breaks the ice, and the conversation follows naturally. The court is simply where the relationship begins.

Tournament format

A format where everyone plays.

Thirty-two players across eight courts, in a Swiss-style tournament of seven rounds. Winners meet winners; players with similar results meet each other. There is no early elimination — everyone plays throughout the day, right up to the championship final.

32 · 8
